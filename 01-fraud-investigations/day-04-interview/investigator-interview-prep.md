# Week 1 Day 4 — Fraud Investigator Interview Prep

## Interview Focus
Week 1 Day 4 focuses on behavioral interview preparation using STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) responses based on real investigative experience.

## Core Positioning
- Approximately 8 years of loss prevention experience
- Investigating internal and external theft and fraud-related activity
- Evidence-based investigative approach
- Surveillance, transaction review, pattern recognition, case development, and law-enforcement coordination
- Bachelor’s degree in Cybersecurity
- Transitioning investigative experience into fraud, claims/SIU, compliance, and related investigative roles

## Tell Me About Yourself
> I am a loss prevention professional with about eight years of experience. Throughout my career, I have been involved in investigating internal and external theft, including gift card fraud, return fraud, ticket switching, and organized retail crime. I am interested in fraud investigations because I enjoy solving complex problems, and I believe one of my strengths is following the evidence and letting it guide the investigation. I’m making this transition because I want the opportunity to apply those investigative skills to more complex financial and compliance-related cases. I also have a Bachelor’s degree in Cybersecurity, which gives me an additional technical foundation that I can bring to investigative work.

## Interview Themes
1. Follow the evidence — suspicion starts an investigation; evidence supports the conclusion.
2. Avoid tunnel vision — consider competing explanations before reaching a finding.
3. Corroborate information — use multiple evidence sources when available.
4. Document what the evidence supports — do not force a conclusion when evidence is insufficient.
5. Use investigative judgment — identify evidence gaps and determine the next investigative step.
6. Translate existing experience — connect loss prevention investigations to broader fraud-investigation competencies.

## Week 1 STAR Story Bank
- Story 1 — Organized Retail Crime Investigation
- Story 2 — Markdown / Ticket-Switching Investigation
- Story 3 — Gift Card Investigation

## Interview Rule
Do not claim that a suspicious pattern proves misconduct. Explain what raised the concern, what evidence was collected, what competing explanations were considered, and why the final conclusion was supported by the evidence.
