> Follow the evidence — do not let suspicion become the conclusion.

The stories demonstrate that an investigator can:
- identify red flags,
- develop competing explanations,
- gather and compare evidence,
- recognize evidence gaps,
- corroborate allegations,
- and document an appropriate disposition when evidence is insufficient.
