# Fraud, Claims & Compliance Investigator Intensive

A 4-week hands-on investigation portfolio focused on **fraud investigations, claims/SIU investigations, and compliance investigations** through realistic case analysis, investigative reporting, evidence evaluation, and interview preparation.

## Project Purpose

This project is designed to build practical, job-relevant knowledge for roles such as:

- Fraud Investigator
- Claims / SIU Investigator
- Compliance Investigator
- AML / Financial Crimes Investigator
- Investigative Analyst

The emphasis is on **investigative judgment and documentation**, not introductory loss-prevention investigations. The project uses fictional cases so the work can be shared publicly as a portfolio.

## Learning Structure

Each week follows the same four-day cycle:

| Day | Focus | What I Will Produce |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | **LEARN** | Domain notes, terminology, workflows, evidence concepts, and flashcards |
| Day 2 | **APPLY** | Investigation scenarios, triage decisions, evidence selection, and analysis |
| Day 3 | **CASE LAB** | A realistic fictional investigation and professional case report |
| Day 4 | **INTERVIEW PRACTICE** | Mock investigator interview, challenge questions, critique, and reflection |

**Rule:** The curriculum is completed in order. Case labs are not started before their assigned day.

## 4-Week Curriculum

### Week 1 — Fraud Investigations

**Goal:** Learn how professional fraud investigators identify, triage, analyze, investigate, document, and disposition suspected fraud.

- Day 1 — Fraud investigator role, investigation lifecycle, fraud types and schemes, indicators/red flags, alerts and referrals, fraud scoring, predication, intake/triage, hypotheses, evidence sources, escalation, disposition.
- Day 2 — Apply concepts to transaction fraud, account takeover, application fraud, employee fraud, and first-party fraud.
- Day 3 — **CASE 001 — Suspicious Transactions**. Complete a Fraud Investigation Report using a fictional case file.
- Day 4 — Mock Fraud Investigator interview and reflection.

### Week 2 — Claims Investigations

**Goal:** Learn claims/SIU investigation work and recognize common claims-fraud indicators.

- Day 1 — Claims investigator role, SIU vs. ordinary claims handling, misrepresentation, staged losses, exaggerated claims, suspicious injury claims, duplicate claims, prior-loss analysis, inconsistencies, documentation analysis, medical documentation basics, provider red flags, and referral criteria.
- Day 2 — Apply concepts to inconsistent claimant statements, repeated claims, unusual provider billing, suspicious documentation, and suspicious claims with innocent explanations.
- Day 3 — **CASE 002 — Suspicious Injury Claim**. Complete a claims/SIU investigation using a fictional case file.
- Day 4 — Mock Claims/SIU Investigator interview and reflection.

### Week 3 — Compliance Investigations

**Goal:** Build foundational corporate and financial compliance investigation skills.

- Day 1 — Code of conduct, employee misconduct, policy violations, conflicts of interest, vendor misconduct, expense fraud, gifts/entertainment, confidential information, whistleblower allegations, retaliation concerns, AML, KYC, CDD, EDD, transaction monitoring, sanctions screening, OFAC concepts, beneficial ownership, customer risk, and AML red flags.
- Day 2 — Apply concepts to conflict of interest, expense fraud, AML concerns, sanctions-screening concerns, and whistleblower allegations.
- Day 3 — **CASE 003 — Compliance Investigation**. Complete a fictional vendor approval/conflict investigation.
- Day 4 — Mock Compliance/AML Investigator interview and reflection.

### Week 4 — Integrated Investigation & Capstone

**Goal:** Combine fraud, claims, and compliance investigation skills into one professional investigative workflow.

- Day 1 — Investigative planning, case strategy, hypothesis testing, evidence matrices, chronologies, link analysis, pattern analysis, corroboration, contradiction analysis, evidence gaps, root cause, findings, substantiation, disposition, escalation, and reporting.
- Day 2 — Complete a complex multi-dimensional investigation simulation.
- Day 3 — **CASE 004 — Integrated Investigation**. Independently define the allegation and scope, develop hypotheses, build an evidence plan, analyze the timeline, evaluate contradictions and alternative explanations, make findings, and produce the final investigative report.
- Day 4 — Final investigator panel interview and portfolio review.

## Investigation Standards

This project will consistently apply the following professional investigation principles:

- Suspicion is not proof.
- Access does not establish responsibility.
- A red flag does not automatically mean fraud or misconduct.
- Investigators should test competing hypotheses and consider innocent explanations.
- Evidence should be documented and tied to the investigative question it addresses.
- Contradictory or missing evidence should be identified rather than ignored.
- Findings should be supported by the available evidence.
- Case disposition should match the strength of the evidence.
- When evidence is insufficient, **inconclusive / further investigation required** is a valid professional conclusion.

## Success Criteria

By the end of the intensive, the portfolio should demonstrate the ability to:

1. Explain the investigation lifecycle across fraud, claims/SIU, and compliance contexts.
2. Recognize domain-specific fraud and compliance indicators.
3. Triage allegations and determine investigative priorities.
4. Identify and request appropriate evidence.
5. Build timelines and analyze contradictions and patterns.
6. Evaluate alternative explanations and evidence gaps.
7. Document findings in a professional investigative report.
8. Explain escalation and disposition decisions.
9. Defend investigative reasoning in an interview setting.
10. Present the completed GitHub repository as a practical investigation portfolio.
